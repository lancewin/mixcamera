Mix Camera Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-25

In one sentence: no account, no uploads, no ads, no third-party analytics SDK. Your photos, videos, and scripts live only on your device; only looking up a watermark address and loading these policy pages need a network connection.

1. What We Collect

We do not collect: the app requires no account or sign-in, and does not collect your phone number, email, contacts, location history, or device identifiers for profiling.

No integrations: the app contains no third-party ad SDKs and no analytics SDKs for behavioural profiling.

The app has no server of its own, and your usage data is never sent back to us.

2. Photos and Videos

Capturing, scanning, and all image processing (watermarks, mosaic, GIF conversion, compositing, and so on) happen entirely on your device.

Files are written to your Photos library only when you tap save. Add Photos Only permission is enough — Full Access is not required.

We do not read anything else in your library. If you pick an item in Works to process, it is read on-device only.

3. Location

The capture watermark can show where a photo was taken. Location is requested only while you use that feature, is used solely to render the watermark text, is never uploaded, and no location history is kept.

Resolving coordinates into an address needs a network connection; the coordinates are then handled by Apple's geocoding service, with no identity information attached. You can opt out: choose Coordinates only or Custom Text in Me → Watermark Content, or simply turn Show Location off.

You can also revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings; the watermark then omits the address, and everything else keeps working.

4. Microphone

Recording audio and the teleprompter's follow-along recognition need microphone access. Follow-along recognition runs on your device and the audio is not uploaded.

Voice-Follow mode only measures volume on-device; Constant mode does not use the microphone at all.

5. Camera

Camera access is used for preview and capture, and only while you are using a capture feature.

This app never accesses the camera in the background.

6. Purchase Information

Purchases go through Apple in-app purchase (StoreKit). Payment methods and card numbers are handled by Apple — we neither receive nor store them.

Purchase receipts are verified locally on your device; we never receive your payment details.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If a guardian becomes aware of any such issue, please contact us at the email below and we will address it.

8. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Your data stays on your own device, so you are always in control: photos and PDFs live in the Photos library or in Works → Documents inside the app, and you can delete them there directly.

Deleting the app removes all local data, including scripts and scan archives kept inside the app. Files already saved to the Photos library must be deleted from Photos.

You can revoke camera, microphone, Photos, and location permissions at any time in iOS Settings; the matching features stop working, and nothing else is affected.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the last-updated date at the top of this document will be revised; please refer to the latest version.

Continuing to use the app means you accept the updated privacy policy.

This policy describes how the current version handles data and does not constitute a promise or guarantee about future versions or long-term consistency.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy or your personal information, or wish to exercise your rights, please email lancewin@foxmail.com.
