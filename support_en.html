FAQ

No sound in my videos?

First allow the app to use the microphone in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone, and keep the mic switch at the top of the capture screen on. With the teleprompter camera, check that you did not tap mute by accident.

The watermark has no address, or the address is wrong?

An address needs location permission and a network connection so iOS can resolve coordinates. If you picked Coordinates only in Me → Watermark Content, you will see coordinates instead of an address. Prefer not to use location? Switch to Custom Text and type it yourself.

Saved but cannot find it in Photos?

Add Photos Only is enough — Full Access is not required. Files appear in Photos → Recents. Document scans export as PDF; find them in Works → Documents.

Follow-along cannot track where I am reading?

Recognition runs entirely on-device. It works best in a quiet room, at normal volume, in Mandarin. If it keeps struggling, switch the mode to Voice-Follow or Constant — neither relies on speech recognition.

Is Pro still mine after switching phones or reinstalling?

Yes. Pro is tied to your Apple ID — tap Restore Purchases on the paywall after switching or reinstalling. If you are on the weekly plan, make sure the subscription is still active.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel in iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions, or tap Manage Subscription on the paywall. You keep access until the end of the current period. Cancelling mid-period is not refunded — refunds are handled by Apple.

How do I delete all my data?

This app has no account and never uploads; photos and scripts live only on this device. Deleting the app removes all local data. You can also clear scratch files first in Me → Storage.
